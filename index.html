A garden for my purple flower🫵

Lately, I find myself thinking about you more often than I expected. Somehow, even on ordinary days, something always reminds me of you.

Your smile, the little moments I see you around campus, and even the times I don't see you somehow stay in my mind.

Just a small thing I made because I wanted to make you smile. 🌷

🌺

Why a Garden? 🌸

I've always thought flowers were beautiful because they don't all bloom at the same time.

Some grow quickly, some take their time, and some bloom when you least expect it.

I guess that's what made me think of you.

There's just something about your smile that stayed in my mind. 🌷

🌸

For You 💜

I don't know where this little story is supposed to go, and I'm not asking you to figure that out either.

I just wanted you to know that meeting you, even from a distance, became one of those little things that made my days more interesting.

Your smile caught my attention first, but getting to know even a little about you made me appreciate you more.

🌷

Just This 💗

So here's a little garden of flowers that can't say anything for me.

I hope they can at least make you smile. 🌸

That's all.

I just wanted to give you something beautiful. 💜
